# 🧾 Every cancelled order tells a business story.

A business intelligence project analyzing revenue leakage at The Souled Store through cancellations, COD orders, discount erosion, delivery inefficiencies, and product-level performance.

Built using SQL, Python, and Power BI, this project transforms raw transactional data into operational insights and strategic recommendations focused on improving profitability and customer experience.

⚠️ Important: This project uses completely synthetic/self-generated data created solely for portfolio and educational purposes.  
The dataset does not represent actual business operations, customers, or financial records of The Souled Store.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data from The Souled Store to uncover hidden revenue leakages across cancellations, discounts, COD orders, product returns, and delivery operations.

The goal is to identify where profitability is being impacted, quantify operational inefficiencies, and provide actionable business recommendations backed by data.

---

# ❓ Problem Statement

How can operational and transactional data be analyzed to identify revenue leakage and improve profitability across e-commerce order processing, discounts, COD operations, and customer fulfilment?

# 💡 Key Findings

- ₹5.35M revenue lost through cancelled orders
- COD contributes disproportionately to cancellation losses
- 88%+ of orders carry discounts
- Instagram generated the highest revenue contribution
- Originals franchise emerged as the top-performing theme
- High discount bands significantly compress profit margins
- Certain high-revenue SKUs also show elevated return risk

---

# 📢 Business Recommendations

### Reduce COD Leakage
Restrict COD availability in high-cancellation regions or introduce prepaid deposits for risky pin codes.

### Optimize Discount Strategy
Avoid excessive discounting on high-margin products and reserve aggressive discounts for clearance inventory.

### Scale High-Performing Channels
Increase focus on Instagram and Influencer campaigns, which contribute significantly to GMV.

### Improve Customer Retention
Strengthen post-purchase CRM flows to improve repeat purchases and reduce dependency on paid acquisition.

### Improve Operational Efficiency
Track delivery partner performance and reduce return/cancellation triggers operationally.
